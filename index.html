About Me

Hello! I am Kene David Nwosu, an R Developer, Data Analyst, and Educator currently based in Geneva, Switzerland. I am pursuing my PhD in epidemiology at the University of Geneva, where my research focuses on infectious diseases and mathematical modeling.

My background bridges data science and web development. I am an expert in R and highly proficient in Python and JavaScript. I am passionate about accessible digital education, which led me to co-found and co-direct The GRAPH Courses, a non-profit platform providing high-quality data science training globally.

About The Course

"Generative AI for Work & Research" is an immersive 8-week program by The GRAPH Courses. It focuses on using no-code AI tools for responsible task automation, scientific communication, and data analysis.

Document Automation

Generating structured research documents using Markdown, LaTeX, and intelligent HTML outputs.

Automated Analysis

Processing datasets transparently using ChatGPT Code Interpreter, Julius, and automated dashboards.

Custom Chatbots

Building interactive assistants and RAG systems tailored for specific research support tasks.

Scientific Outreach

Designing accurate visuals and automated summaries for clear, accessible research dissemination.
